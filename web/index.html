Available measurements

VBG

Enter at least one value. Any two of pH, PvCO₂, and blood-gas HCO₃ allow algebraic completion. Measured pH and PvCO₂ also enable a best-guess arterial interpretation; optional saturation switches the CO₂ estimate to Farkas.

If reported standard base excess is absent, sufficient gas values enable calculated venous SBE, assuming normothermia (37°C).

Optional CMP/BMP

Na, Cl, and serum total CO₂ enable anion gap; albumin adds its correction. Same-time chemistry can add a venous Stewart partition.

Serum total CO₂ is distinct from blood-gas HCO₃. Lactate remains a measured chemistry value.
