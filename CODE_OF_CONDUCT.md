# Community conduct

Participation in this project should be respectful, evidence-aware, and safe for a public
research-software space.

- Discuss people and ideas with professional respect.
- Critique claims, methods, and code with specific evidence rather than personal attacks.
- Do not post PHI, real patient values, credentials, protected records, or restricted source
  material.
- Do not present the Explorer or project discussion as patient-specific medical advice.
- Respect the evidence, provenance, licensing, and intended-use boundaries documented in the
  repository.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these boundaries and may limit
participation when needed to protect people or data. Report non-sensitive project concerns through
the issue forms. Report sensitive security/privacy concerns through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/reblocke/VBG_interpreter/security/advisories/new),
not a public issue.
